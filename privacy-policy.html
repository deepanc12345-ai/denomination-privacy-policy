Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

DLCK NETWORK ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Denomination Calculator mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information You Enter in the App

Denomination Calculator allows you to enter counts for currency denominations such as ₹500, ₹200, ₹100, ₹50, ₹20, ₹10, ₹5, ₹2 and ₹1. These calculation values are used to calculate totals, counts and amounts in words.

The app does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, phone number, contacts, precise location, or government identification information.

2. Local App Data

Calculation history and app settings are designed to be stored locally on your Android device for the app's functionality. DLCK NETWORK does not operate a separate server for storing your calculation history.

Local app data can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application, subject to the normal behavior of the Android operating system.

3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or share certain information for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes. Depending on Google's current SDK behavior and your device settings, this can include information such as IP address, advertising/device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic information.

This information is handled by Google according to Google's applicable policies and settings. For more information, please review Google's privacy information: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. Data handled by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads is subject to the security practices of those providers. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Calculation information stored locally by the app remains on your device until you remove it through the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. DLCK NETWORK does not maintain a separate online database of your calculation history.

Data handled by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices and applicable controls.

6. Children's Privacy

Denomination Calculator is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

7. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), a third-party advertising service. Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. You should review Google's privacy documentation for details about its services.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be posted at this webpage with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Privacy Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning Denomination Calculator, users may contact the developer through the developer contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.
